Services

How an engagement works

Simple enough to start small.

A pilot can begin with a limited procedure set or a sample of your existing documentation before a wider rollout.

  1. 1
    Discover

    Understand your users, platforms, support tiers, tools and current knowledge sources.

  2. 2
    Assess

    Identify risk, duplication, missing controls and the highest-value documentation work.

  3. 3
    Build

    Create or remediate the procedures and configure the private knowledge experience where required.

  4. 4
    Handover

    Deliver the runbooks, governance outputs, deployment documentation and next review plan.
