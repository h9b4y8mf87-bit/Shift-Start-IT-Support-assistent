Quality gate

  • No procedure, symptom, cause or command article may disappear from the generated catalogue.
  • Duplicate taxonomy categories and known malformed generated titles fail validation.
  • Verified procedures require a named reviewer, a passed quality gate and non-generated technical content.
  • High-reuse generic commands are reported, while mismatched commands in verified content fail the build.
  • The multi-select wizard prioritises verified procedures and clearly warns when only under-review guidance matches.
  • The ITSM button remains disabled until an administrator provides an explicit ticket URL template.
