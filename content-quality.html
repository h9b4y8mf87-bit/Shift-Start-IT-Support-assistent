Knowledge governance

Content quality and assurance

Every procedure remains available, but operational trust is explicit. Verification v2 is authoritative: a procedure is shown as Verified only when the recorded governance state, evidence-complete gate and promotion-readiness gate all agree. Historical verified labels that do not meet the current gate are shown as Revalidation Required.

Quality gate

  • No procedure, symptom, cause or command article may disappear from the generated catalogue.
  • Duplicate taxonomy categories and known malformed generated titles fail validation.
  • Verified procedures require the authoritative verification-v2 state, complete evidence and promotion readiness; a historical label alone is never sufficient.
  • High-reuse generic commands are reported while the affected guidance remains under review or revalidation.
  • The multi-select wizard, search data and enterprise catalogue expose the same effective governance status.
  • The ITSM button remains disabled until an administrator provides an explicit ticket URL template.
