Purpose & operating model

About ShiftStart

ShiftStart is designed as a technician-first, evidence-based operational knowledge system—not a generic documentation archive.

01

Find

Search by symptom, error code, task or domain, or use multi-symptom triage.

02

Assess

Check risk level, support tier, owner and review status before acting.

03

Resolve

Follow evidence, safety, diagnostic, remediation and rollback guidance.

04

Verify

Confirm the expected outcome and document evidence.

05

Escalate

Hand over with context, evidence and a clear resolver-team path.
